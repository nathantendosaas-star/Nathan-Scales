# Outreach Playbook — Kampala/Wakiso Construction & Roofing Leads
**11 leads · Digital-only channels: WhatsApp DM, WhatsApp Voice Call, Email**

---

## How to Use This Playbook

**Uganda B2B norms baked into every sequence:**
- WhatsApp is the default professional channel here — more reliable than email for a first read. Email is reserved for anything that reads as "official": donor pre-qualification, formal proposals, procurement.
- Address people by title on first contact — "Sir/Madam," "Managing Director," "Eng." — not first names, until they set the tone.
- Send window: **Mon–Fri, 9:00–11:30am or 2:00–4:30pm EAT.** Skip Monday before 9am (internal meetings) and Friday after 3pm. Never Sunday.
- Keep WhatsApp messages to 3–5 short lines. Long paragraphs read as spam on mobile.
- **Cadence:** Day 0 (Touch 1) → Day 3 (Touch 2, different channel) → Day 6–7 (Touch 3, value-add) → Day 12–14 (Touch 4, break-up). Don't chase daily — it reads as desperate and burns the number.
- Every opener uses a *specific, true* detail from the evidence column (a real client name, a real cert, a real technical gap) — never generic flattery. This is what makes it feel researched instead of copy-pasted.
- **No pricing in Touch 1 or 2.** Ugandan procurement culture negotiates by call or in person; naming a number too early kills the conversation before it starts.
- Every CTA is low-friction: a 10–15 minute call, never "sign up" or "buy now."
- **Data gaps:** 4 of the 11 leads have no WhatsApp number or public email on file. For those, Touch 0 is a short reconnaissance step (checking the site's contact page, or one brief front-desk call to ask which WhatsApp number reaches the decision-maker) before the sequence below can run. Flagged clearly under each.
