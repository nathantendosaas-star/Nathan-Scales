# Nathan Scales — 3-Year Growth Strategy
### Full Brezscales Playbook Clone — Adapted for Web/Lead-Gen/Software (Not Ads)

This mirrors the structure of `brezscales.md` section-for-section — content architecture, course funnel, brand separation, SaaS layer, revenue evolution — with the mechanics swapped from **paid-ads freelancing** to **AI-powered lead-gen/web-build agency work**, and an education arm added on the same timeline Brez actually ran his (agency-first, content around month ~10–14, bootcamp soon after, high-ticket mentorship in Year 2, SaaS + free front-end pivot in Year 3).

**The education product you'll be selling:** how to build and land clients for AI-assisted lead-capture systems, websites, and small automations for local/regional businesses — the exact system Nathan Scales runs, taught to other aspiring builders. This is your "freelance brand scaling" equivalent.

Same separation-of-purpose logic as Brez's main-vs-@roasbrez split: the personal channel builds the brand and audience: it should never carry a hard pitch. The Academy channel exists purely to qualify people who already want to learn the skill.

**Native-content philosophy (carried over as-is — it's platform-agnostic):**
1. Content should look like content, not an ad — screen recordings, phone-shot, real workflow, not polished corporate video.
2. Hook in the first 2–3 seconds.
3. Lead with the outcome ("client's lead form was live in 48 hours"), explain the method after.
4. High-volume testing on hooks/formats once posting daily.

## The Education Funnel (TOFU → MOFU → BOFU)

### TOFU — Free attention
Nathan Tendo build-in-public content + Nathan Scales Academy tutorials. Cost to viewer: $0.

### MOFU — Low-ticket entry: Lead Capture Agency Bootcamp
- **Price:** $47–97
- **Format:** 5-day live virtual training
- **Content:** niche selection, the risk-reversal offer script (deferred fee + finder's fee), building a basic lead-capture funnel with AI tools, first-client outreach
- Functions the same way Brez's bootcamp did: builds desire and demonstrates competence, then closes into the Round Table. Don't hide that — be upfront that it's a taste of the full system.

### BOFU — High-ticket close: Builder's Round Table
- **Price:** ~$2,000–4,000, invite-only, not publicly listed
- **Format:** group mentorship, hot-seat feedback, direct access, daily/weekly calls
- **Sells via:** case-study-driven VSL + application call, not a checkout page
- **Positioning:** exclusive, not expensive — "apply," not "buy"

### Revenue math to expect
Digital education product margins run ~95% with no fulfillment cost. One Round Table sale is worth roughly 20–80 bootcamp sales depending on final pricing — so bootcamp volume matters even though it's not the main revenue line.

## Year 1 — Foundation & First Revenue (Months 1–12)

> Theme: same as Brez's Year 1 — do the work, get results, zero content pressure yet.

### Q1: Land the first client
- Reply to current responses; push Touch 2–4 on the remaining Kampala/Wakiso leads.
- Test the risk-reversal offer: reduced/deferred build fee + finder's fee on closed contracts.
- Track one metric from day one — this becomes your proof line for both future clients *and* the eventual bootcamp pitch.

### Q2: Systematize + second niche
- GitHub Actions lead-review automation live; use it to qualify a second construction-adjacent niche.
- Update site description to "all-around marketing by niche," same core service.
- Fold a lighter Jarvis into Nathan Scales as an internal ops tool — this is the seed of the Year-3 SaaS product, started early rather than bolted on late.

### Q3–Q4: Stack 3–5 clients, soft-launch content
- Target 3–5 signed clients.
- **Start Nathan Tendo build-in-public content** — pure journey documentation, no pitch: landing the first client, building the lead system, the actual AI-assisted workflow. This is earlier than Brez's own content start relative to his agency, intentionally — you want an audience warming up before the bootcamp launches in Year 2 Q1–Q2.
- Lock the brand split now: Nathan Tendo (personal) vs. Nathan Scales (agency) vs. reserve the Academy handle.

**Year 1 exit targets:** 3–5 paying clients · one repeatable proof metric · lead qualification automated · brand split live · early build-in-public audience started.

## Year 2 — Audience Growth & Education Launch (Months 13–24)

> Theme: turn results into content, turn content into inbound, launch the education arm.

### Q1 (Months 13–15): Content engine + Academy channel
- Increase posting to 2–4 short-form/day across platforms on Nathan Tendo.
- Launch **Nathan Scales Academy** as the secondary, pure-tutorial channel.
- Weekly long-form (YouTube/other) documenting business + build tutorials.
- Develop your hook vocabulary: bold claims, before/afters, contrarian takes on "no-code vs AI-built."

### Q2 (Months 16–18): Funnel construction + Bootcamp launch
- Free lead magnet: short case-study/mini-guide ("how we cut lead response time by X%"), gated behind email/WhatsApp.
- **Launch the Lead Capture Agency Bootcamp** ($47–97, 5-day live).
- Start an email/SMS nurture sequence pushing bootcamp attendees toward the (soon-to-launch) Round Table.
- Keep Academy purely educational — no hard pitch until the nurture sequence.

### Q3–Q4 (Months 19–24): Round Table launch + scaling
- **Launch the Builder's Round Table** ($2,000–4,000, application-only, weekly calls).
- Start a small bounty/clipper program — early students or clients repost build-in-public content for a per-view payout. 5–10 people is enough to start.
- Plan 1–2 higher-visibility content moments (a real launch, a big before/after, a public build challenge) — the goal is narrative tension, not manufactured drama.
- Keep growing the agency side in parallel — target 8–15 active clients across niches.

**Year 2 exit targets:** bootcamp + Round Table both running · 50K–200K combined followers · 5,000–20,000-lead email/WhatsApp list · 5–10 clippers · 8–15 agency clients.

## Year 3 — Platform & Recurring Revenue (Months 25–36)

> Theme: shift from one-time payments (agency builds, bootcamp, Round Table) to recurring revenue. Build the tool. Become the platform.

### Q1 (Months 25–27): Productize Jarvis
- Package Jarvis as a required subscription tool for Round Table students and retainer clients — lead review, follow-up automation, reporting.
- Price as a subscription ($97–197/month), bundled into delivery rather than sold separately — this is what makes it mandatory recurring revenue instead of an optional add-on.

### Q2 (Months 28–30): Free front-end pivot
- Shift the bootcamp/entry-level training to free, massively increasing top-of-funnel volume.
- Gate the Round Table behind an application + call instead of a fixed price, giving you pricing flexibility as demand grows.
- Scale Nathan Tendo + Academy combined reach toward 500K+.

### Q3–Q4 (Months 31–36): Full ecosystem
- Bring on 2–4 coaches/mentors to run Round Table calls and student support, removing you from fulfillment.
- Add usage-based pricing on top of Jarvis's base subscription for higher-tier automation/AI features.
- Four distinct, mutually-protecting brands fully separated: **Nathan Tendo** (personal/content) → **Nathan Scales Academy** (education) → **Nathan Scales** (agency/client work) → **Jarvis** (SaaS).
- Cross-pollination loop: content drives bootcamp signups → bootcamp/Round Table students use Jarvis → Jarvis-powered client results become new case studies → case studies become new content.

**Year 3 exit targets:** recurring SaaS revenue is the largest single line · multiple niches served off one repeatable system · Jarvis generating income independent of your time · a coaching team handling education fulfillment.

## Brand Separation Strategy

| Brand | Purpose | Insulation |
|---|---|---|
| **Nathan Tendo** | Personal authority, build-in-public content | If this brand takes a hit, the agency and education businesses are insulated |
| **Nathan Scales (agency)** | Done-for-you client work | Clean, minimal, application-only — a client complaint doesn't splash onto the personal brand |
| **Nathan Scales Academy** | Bootcamp + Round Table | Separate audience expectation (learning) from client-service expectation (delivery) |
| **Jarvis** | SaaS tool | Can eventually be licensed to *other* small agencies too, independent of the Nathan Scales name entirely |

Decide the handles now while it's cheap — Brez's own rebrand (Brez Marketing → Brandlab) happened *after* the agency already had reputation attached to the wrong name, which made it a harder move than doing it early.

## Caveats before you run this

- **The rev-share/finder's-fee terms need to be airtight** on what counts as "a result" — a qualified lead vs. a signed contract — since your deliverable is a step removed from revenue in a way ad spend isn't.
- **Your bootcamp/Round Table audience is different from Brez's.** He sold to 18–25-year-olds chasing lifestyle content. Your buyers are more likely aspiring builders/freelancers who respond to real before/afters and working code, not luxury flexes — keep the content proof-driven rather than copying the lifestyle angle wholesale.
- **Don't launch the Round Table before you have real client case studies.** Brez's own reviewers flag this as the weak point of his funnel — a high-ticket program sold before the underlying method is proven creates a credibility gap. Your Year 1 agency work is what makes the Year 2 education launch legitimate instead of hollow.
- **Treat Brez's self-reported revenue and follower numbers as pacing inspiration, not a target** — they're unverified and from a much larger, English-speaking-US-first audience than your starting point.
