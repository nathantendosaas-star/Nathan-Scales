# Multi-Touch Cold Outreach & Sales Sequences

This playbook defines standardized plain text and hybrid markdown/email sequences for Nathan Scales Marketing Agency AI agents.

---

## 4-Touch Sequence Framework

### Touch 1: Research Observation & Initial Hook (Day 0)

**Goal:** Establish immediate relevance using specific evidence from the prospect's public presence.
**Channel:** Email or Direct Message (WhatsApp / LinkedIn)

**Subject Line Options:**
- `digital standards for {{COMPANY_NAME}}`
- `quick note regarding {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s website`
- `donor pre-qualification audit — {{COMPANY_NAME}}`
- `{{COMPANY_NAME}} site performance`

**Body Template:**
```text
Good {{TIME_OF_DAY}}, {{SALUTATION_NAME}}.

I noticed {{COMPANY_NAME}} handles {{NOTABLE_PROJECTS_OR_CUSTOMERS}} — but {{SPECIFIC_DIGITAL_GAP_OBSERVATION}} (e.g. your website is running an older HTML build without HTTPS / mobile responsiveness / dedicated compliance access).

For a firm operating at your level, that's often the first thing an international procurement or client team checks during shortlisting.

I perform concise digital audits for {{INDUSTRY_CATEGORY}} firms — I'd be happy to share 3 specific findings on your current setup. Would that be useful?

Best regards,

{{SENDER_NAME}}
Nathan Scales Marketing Agency
https://nathanscales.vercel.app/
```

---

### Touch 2: Channel Shift / Direct Call Follow-Up (Day 3)

**Goal:** Provide context and offer a quick 10-minute conversational walkthrough.

**Subject Line:** `Re: digital standards for {{COMPANY_NAME}}`

**Body Template:**
```text
Following up on my note, {{SALUTATION_NAME}}.

I put together a fast review of {{WEBSITE_URL}} specifically looking at:
1. {{AUDIT_POINT_1}} (e.g. Mobile page load and asset rendering)
2. {{AUDIT_POINT_2}} (e.g. Centralized compliance & safety documentation pathways)
3. {{AUDIT_POINT_3}} (e.g. Direct RFP and inquiry route)

Would you or your team have 10 minutes this Thursday or Friday for a brief call to go over these?

Best,

{{SENDER_NAME}}
Nathan Scales Marketing Agency
```

---

### Touch 3: Free Value-Add / Mini Breakdown (Day 7)

**Goal:** Deliver immediate value upfront without asking for a commitment.

**Subject Line:** `3 quick findings for {{COMPANY_NAME}}`

**Body Template:**
```text
Hi {{SALUTATION_NAME}},

Rather than waiting for a call, I wanted to share the 3 quick findings from our audit of {{WEBSITE_URL}}:

- Finding 1: {{DETAILED_FINDING_1}}
- Finding 2: {{DETAILED_FINDING_2}}
- Finding 3: {{DETAILED_FINDING_3}}

If you'd like us to draft a quick 1-page proposal or fix plan to address these, let me know and I'll send it right over.

Best,

{{SENDER_NAME}}
Nathan Scales Marketing Agency
```

---

### Touch 4: Polite Break-up Email (Day 12-14)

**Goal:** Respectfully close the thread while leaving the door open for future priority shifts.

**Subject Line:** `Closing the loop — {{COMPANY_NAME}}`

**Body Template:**
```text
Hi {{SALUTATION_NAME}},

I'll leave this here for now.

If upgrading {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s digital presence or pre-qualification readiness becomes a priority ahead of your next tender or campaign, feel free to reach back out.

Wishing you and the {{COMPANY_NAME}} team continued success.

Kind regards,

{{SENDER_NAME}}
Nathan Scales Marketing Agency
https://nathanscales.vercel.app/
```
