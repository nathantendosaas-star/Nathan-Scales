# Cold Outreach Frameworks & Strategy Guide

This guide compiles strategic copywriting frameworks, subject line rules, and follow-up principles for sales outreach.

---

## Copywriting Frameworks

### 1. Observation → Problem → Proof → Ask
- **Observation:** "I noticed {{COMPANY_NAME}}'s website still runs on a legacy HTML build..."
- **Problem:** "...which means international donor panels looking for HSE/ISO documentation during pre-qualification may pass over your profile."
- **Proof:** "We perform digital audits for top-tier contractors to align their public face with procurement standards."
- **Ask:** "Would a short 3-point audit be useful to your team?"

### 2. Trigger → Insight → Low Friction CTA
- **Trigger:** "Saw that {{COMPANY_NAME}} just launched a regional expansion in {{REGION}}."
- **Insight:** "Growth at this stage usually exposes gaps between sales material and web conversion pathways."
- **Ask:** "Open to a quick 10-minute call to walk through a few quick wins?"

---

## Subject Line Rules

- Keep them short (2–4 words).
- Use lowercase or natural title case.
- Avoid promotional hype words (e.g., "FREE", "BEST DEAL", "GUARANTEED", "100%").
- Make it look like an internal work email from a colleague.

---

## Follow-up Principles

1. **Add fresh value in every follow-up:** Never say "Just checking in" or "Bumping this up."
2. **Space touchpoints wisely:** Day 0 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 12.
3. **Respect the breakup:** The final touchpoint must explicitly state you will close the loop and leave the door open.
